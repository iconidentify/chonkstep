# ChonkStep 0.7.2

A startup display fix, polkit dialogs, and Dock and Clip fixes since 0.7.1.
Published through the `preview-v0.7.2` prerelease channel.

## Highlights

- **Displays at startup:** the session watches udev for display changes
  before it reads the connectors for the first time. A display that comes up
  a moment after that read, as a monitor behind a Thunderbolt dock can, is
  adopted through the usual hotplug rescan instead of staying dark until it
  is unplugged and plugged in again.
- **Dialogs:** a layer surface that names no output, such as a polkit
  prompt, opens on the focused output. Layer-shell version 5 clients, such as
  hyprtoolkit's dialogs, connect instead of being disconnected.
- **Dock and Clip:** the Dock clock shows local time, and the Clip's
  workspace label scales with its tile (tidux).
- **Build:** chonkstep builds with Rust 1.99.
